
## Monitored return-value gap, explicitly not passed

A separate complete source fixture declares `int HILOG_INFO(...)` and returns
`(HILOG_INFO(..., value), 0)` from an unsigned helper. Built-in comma produces literal
zero, but the existing generic expression may-flow unions the logging operand's material
into the returned status. Both baseline and final direct/Runner report the actual internal
log plus a false material dependency at the caller's numeric-code log (fixture lines 3/6;
semantic expectation line 3 only). Pure literal helper returns do not show this problem.

This is `comma_status_return_mayflow_taints_literal_metadata`, a concrete unchanged
overcandidate in the frozen probe and validation JSON, not a passing test or safety result.
It is not fixed by suppressing all unsigned/status/helper returns: that would discard real
or unknown material. The next step is bounded proof of built-in comma's returned-value role.
