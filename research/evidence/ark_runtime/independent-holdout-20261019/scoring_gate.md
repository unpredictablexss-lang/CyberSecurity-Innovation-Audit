# Fail-closed scoring gate
**BLOCKED_NO_INDEPENDENT_LABELS**. TP/FP/FN/TN and Precision/Recall are not computed; zero FP/FN is prohibited. Score only after frozen rules, independent samples, two eligible human labels, frozen hashes, source-only adjudication and a later sealed prediction join. Per family: TP is adjudicated target mechanism reported; FP is safe/unrelated reported; FN is target mechanism not reported; TN is safe/unrelated not reported. Unknown, partial, failed, not_applicable, unlabelled and unresolved disputed rows are excluded, never TN/FN. Precision=TP/(TP+FP), Recall=TP/(TP+FN); zero denominators are undefined. Any provenance or independence failure yields BLOCKED.
